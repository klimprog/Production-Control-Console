# Production Control Console

*[Русская версия](README.md)*

![Production Control Console](About/GitHub_Preview.jpg)

**Your colony's production management in one window.**

`Version 0.2.6` · `RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7` · requires [RimUI Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763759074)

## Overview

Production Control Console brings your colony's production management into one window. Instead of checking every workbench, stockpile, growing zone, and pen one by one, you can see the whole picture, create production orders, and quickly find out why work is running — or why it has stopped.

The mod does not replace the game's own systems: it places the same bills and designations you would place by hand, arranges them for you, and keeps them from contradicting each other. Your colonists still do the work, following their usual work priorities.

Open the console with the **Production** button on RimWorld's bottom bar.

## Production

- Create orders with a step-by-step wizard: choose the product, amount, ingredient sources, allowed materials, workbenches, workers, and destination stockpile.
- Tick several products in the wizard to create a batch of orders: shared settings are entered once, while each product keeps its own mode and amount. The Use as template button creates a new order with the settings of an existing one.
- Choose a recipe variant: a simple meal one at a time or four at once. Amounts are counted in whole runs, and the field shows the result right away.
- Use one of three straightforward modes: make a fixed amount, maintain a target stock, or produce forever.
- Choose which materials and ingredients may be used, assign specific colonists, or leave the job open to anyone qualified.
- Take ingredients from every stockpile, selected stockpiles, or within a set radius of the workbench.
- Send finished goods to storage or drop them on the floor by the workbench: the crafter starts the next item right away, and other colonists haul the result.
- Compare the material cost of the same recipe across different types of workbenches.
- Multiple workbenches contribute to the same target and share one stock count, preventing each bench from producing unnecessary extras. One workbench can serve several orders, queued by priority.

## Growing

Manage growing zones and hydroponics from a single tab. Keep normal repeat sowing, build a crop queue, or temporarily stop new sowing. In queue mode, a field follows a complete cycle: clear the area, sow the whole field, wait for the crop, harvest it, then move on to the next plant.

You can also manage blight, repeat crop queues, and copy a saved plan to other fields.

## Animals

Manage taming, hunting, and slaughter by animal species. For tamed herds, you can set limits by sex and life stage, choose medicine, and assign an area or pen. The console uses normal RimWorld designations rather than replacing the work system, so your colonists still perform these jobs according to their usual work priorities.

## The whole colony at a glance

- Overview of active, waiting, stopped, and problematic production orders.
- Dedicated pages for workbenches, workers, and stockpiles, with quick navigation between related objects.
- Stock summary, stockpile capacity, and material balance for linked orders.
- Production history and charts covering the last seven in-game days.
- Clear reasons when an order cannot continue: missing materials, workbench, skill, worker, or available stockpile.

## In-game help

The window has a "?" button: more than forty topics in plain language — what each block does, when it is useful, and what the mod deliberately does not do. Any topic can take you straight there: "Show in console" opens the matching tab and highlights the block for a couple of seconds.

## Compatibility

The catalog is built from the game's own data instead of a hard-coded list. Standard recipes and production buildings added by other mods are therefore detected automatically. Production Control Console does not use Harmony.

The mod can be added to an existing save. You do not need to start a new colony.

## Requirements

- **RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7** — the same mod works with both versions: each game loads its own build.
- **[RimUI Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763759074)** 0.4.0 or newer is required and must be placed above Production Control Console in the mod list.

## Before removing the mod

Delete any production orders created through the console before disabling the mod. These orders use a custom bill type and are stored in the save.

The first time you load a save after removing the mod, the log may show harmless errors caused by leftover data. They do not prevent the save from loading and will disappear after you save the game again.
